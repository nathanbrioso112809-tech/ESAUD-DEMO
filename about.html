Try either side of the marketplace

This prototype is for exploring the buyer and farmer experience.

For buyers

  1. Browse or search the sample produce catalog.
  2. Open an item, message a farmer, or add it to your cart.
  3. Enter delivery details and choose a transport option.
  4. Find your order in Profile → To Ship and view its demo tracking timeline.

For farmers

  1. Choose Farmer / Seller when creating a local account.
  2. Add sample products and set a quantity and price.
  3. Update availability in Manage Inventory.
  4. Review buyer orders and coordinate directly with buyers.
ℹ️
About this demo

Profiles, listings, chats, and orders are saved in the current browser only. There is no marketplace server, cross-device sync, payment processing, automatic SMS, or live driver GPS. Sample tracking is illustrative; confirm real availability and delivery directly with the farmer.
